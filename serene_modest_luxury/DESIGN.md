## Brand & Style

The design system embodies "quiet luxury" and understated modest couture. Rooted in artisanal craftsmanship, gentle textures, and architectural discipline, the aesthetic balances serene editorial spreads with the operational rigor required of high-end commerce and inventory dashboards. It bridges the sensory intimacy of fine textiles with the razor-sharp clarity of modern digital design.

The target audience comprises discerning global shoppers seeking sophisticated, modest high-fashion silhouettes, alongside boutique atelier managers tracking bespoke orders, archival collections, and client consultations. 

Drawing from modern **Minimalism** and high-fashion **Editorial Curation**, the interface avoids gratuitous ornamentation. Instead, it prioritizes generous breathing room, precise hairline dividers, organic tactile neutrals, and purposeful hierarchy. The mood is peaceful, dignified, uncompromisingly tailored, and distinctly elevated.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural earth pigments, sun-baked clay, unbleached linen, and botanical foliage. The system relies primarily on natural, low-contrast tonal pairings to cultivate warmth and calm authority without harsh artificial whites or pure blacks.

- **Background & Canvas:** `#FAF8F5` (Warm Bone/Cream) acts as the primary atmospheric foundation, establishing a tactile, paper-like warmth that softens visual fatigue.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** `#F2ECE4` and `#EBE1D7` provide seamless tonal layering for cards, data grids, checkout panels, and filter modules.
- **Typography & Structural Accents:** `#2E221E` (Deep Espresso) replaces standard ink blacks, maintaining high legibility while harmonizing warmly with underlying surfaces. Secondary copy transitions into `#6E635E` for soft contrast.
- **Accents:** `#9F6A54` (Terracotta) functions as the key brand callout—directing intent through interactive buttons, active badge indicators, and highlights. `#737965` (Sage) serves as a stabilizing, contemplative secondary tone for inventory counters, sustainable collection tags, and verified statuses.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Hairline strokes are strictly standardized to `#E6DDD2`, sustaining boundary clarity without cutting up the editorial whitespace.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances high-fashion narrative drama with programmatic clarity:

- **Editorial Headings (Newsreader):** Brings a refined, intellectual literary cadence reminiscent of couture quarterly publications. Display weights favor light and regular cuts with generous line height and gentle kerning to let letterforms evoke luxury. It is preserved for section intros, collection titles, lookbook quotes, and primary KPI values.
- **Interface & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Grounded, modern, and geometrically balanced. It provides pristine optical legibility for customer metrics, SKU tracking, order status, and lengthy garment care treatises.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase applications in `label-md` and `label-sm` must carry deliberate tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.06em - 0.08em`) to mirror luxury catalog taxonomy and clean admin table headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid that shifts dynamically between sweeping editorial compositions and structured dashboard frameworks.

- **Editorial Viewports:** 12-column asymmetric grid with wide gutters (`2.5rem`) and expansive canvas margins (`5rem`). Product showcases favor breathing room over density, using alternating 5-column and 7-column content pairings to echo art-directed magazine spreads.
- **Admin & Operations Viewports:** 12-column structured grid with tighter 1.5rem gutters. Dashboard views utilize fixed 260px left navigation rails with fluid content areas, prioritizing data scanning without compromising aesthetic serenity.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - *Mobile (< 768px):* 4 columns, 1.25rem page margins, collapsed navigation drawers, and stacked product lookbook viewports.
  - *Tablet (768px – 1024px):* 8 columns, 2rem page margins, fluid two-column editorial layouts.
  - *Desktop (> 1024px):* 12 columns, capped at a maximum width of `1440px` centered within warm canvas margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, neon glows, and aggressive 3D projections in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Low-Contrast Outlines**. Visual depth mirrors the tactile stacking of premium bond paper and raw textiles.

- **Base Layer:** The global canvas (#FAF8F5).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Data Panels):** Achieved via background tonal shifts to `#F2ECE4` coupled with a crisp, 1px border of `#E6DDD2`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts, Popovers):** Positioned over base layers with `#FAF8F5`, framed by `#E6DDD2` hairlines, and anchored with an ambient, tinted whisper shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(46, 34, 30, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(46, 34, 30, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Modals, Lookbook Drawers):** Supported by a muted veil (`rgba(46, 34, 30, 0.2)` backdrop) that dims surroundings with a faint 2px blur, ensuring complete focus on garment customization and analytical reports.

## Shapes

The design uses an intentional, sculptural contrast between structural discipline and human ergonomics:

- **Interactive Core (Pill Form):** Primary CTAs, status chips, search bars, and filter toggles adopt full pill radiuses (`rounded-full` / `9999px`). This conveys soft tactility and effortless modesty.
- **Structural Framing:** Content cards, image frames, analytical tables, and dialog sheets use subtle, calibrated curves (`rounded-lg` at 12px–16px) or precise orthogonal bounds to preserve the razor-sharp lines of high-fashion layouts.
- **Focus Rings:** Gentle 2px offset rings tinted with Terracotta Soft (`#A66E58`) ensure accessibility while preserving serene aesthetics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), filled with `#9F6A54` (Terracotta), text in `#FAF8F5`, styled with `label-md`. Hover state transitions to `#A66E58` with subtle 200ms ease. Padding: 12px 28px.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Full pill geometry, background transparent, 1px border in `#2E221E`, text in `#2E221E`. Hover state fills with `#F2ECE4`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, text in `#2E221E` with an animated terracotta underline on hover.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Inputs sit on `#FAF8F5` or `#F2ECE4` with 1px border in `#E6DDD2` and a rounded-full or 10px radius.
- Floating or uppercase structural labels (`label-sm`) in `#6E635E`.
- Active focus smoothly transitions the border to `#9F6A54` with a 2px offset glow in `rgba(159, 106, 84, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Modest, refined status chips using pill geometry.
- **Published / In Stock:** Soft Sage background (`#EAECE4`), text `#737965`.
- **Bespoke / In Review:** Soft Terracotta tint (`rgba(159, 106, 84, 0.08)`), text `#9F6A54`.
- **Category Filter Pill:** `#F2ECE4` base, transitioning to active state `#2E221E` with white text upon selection.

### Cards & Editorial Containers
- **Product Card:** Borderless or faint hairline border (`#E6DDD2`), flush imagery with warm neutral backdrop placeholder (`#F2ECE4`), followed by editorial serif typography for product titles and sans-serif for prices.
- **Dashboard Metric Card:** Background `#F2ECE4`, 1px border `#E6DDD2`, 20px padding, rounded-lg (12px), housing large Newsreader display numbers alongside quiet Plus Jakarta Sans micro-labels.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular radios and softened checkboxes (4px radius) framed in 1.5px `#E6DDD2`.
- Checked state fills with `#9F6A54` featuring an off-white inner mark.

### Data Tables & Archival Lists
- Border-bottom only (`1px solid #E6DDD2`), zero vertical borders.
- Header row set in uppercase `label-sm` with `#6E635E`.
- Hover state on rows applies a quiet tint of `#F2ECE4` across cells.
